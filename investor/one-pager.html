1 Why now

  • Demand is grant-funded and compounding. BUS budget rises 2.4× to 2030, and air-to-air opened up in Apr 2026. No brand owns that category yet.
  • Supply is fragmented. Installers are good engineers but weak marketers, and they lose capacity to surveys, admin and grant paperwork.
  • The buying journey leaks. Slow surveys, opaque quotes, grant forms, no-shows.
  • Incumbent SG&A scales linearly with surveyors, sales, schedulers and back office.
  • National reach alone doesn't win. British Gas (7,000+ engineers) rides its energy customer base. Safestyle's national branches failed in 2023 under fixed cost and CAC.
  • Wedge: air-to-air heating + cooling. It's simpler and more standardisable than air-to-water, so it's the right product to prove the machine on.
Positioning: consumers buy heat pumps and home heating, with cooling as the upsell. The corporate ambition is home electrification: solar, batteries and EV charging on the same OS later.

Target: <15 central FTE running 6 brands at ~3,600 installs/yr. Humans handle exceptions and regulated decisions; agents do the volume.

Fulfilment programme

  • Verified certs, insurance and coverage; a fixed rate card per package, with priced variations.
  • A full job pack before acceptance: kit, photos, routes, electrics, fee. Kit arrives on time; fast payment.
  • Quality scores, rework duty, retention holdback. Goal: our job beats one they find themselves.
  • Partners and Dang It's Hot crews take jobs from the same platform, so cost and quality are benchmarked like for like.
