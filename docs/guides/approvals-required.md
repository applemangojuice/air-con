# Approvals still needed: legal, tax and engineering

Every sign-off a professional (or you) still has to give before or during
the pilots: what it is, why it matters, who gives it, and where the app
records it. The app organises these; it never replaces them. Statutory items
on a job's compliance checklist can't be overridden.

Sources: [../research/uk-consumer-legal.md](../research/uk-consumer-legal.md),
[../research/commissioning-standards.md](../research/commissioning-standards.md),
[../research/README.md](../research/README.md) (the 22 open questions) and
[../pilot-build-plan.md](../pilot-build-plan.md) §6 and §10. The research had
almost no live web access: treat every legal and technical summary as a
working note for the professional to confirm, not advice.

## Before the first install

| # | What | Why | Who | In the app |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | **Company F-gas certificate** (REFCOM or another approved body) | A business installing refrigerant equipment must hold one, as well as its engineers. If Dang It's Hot Ltd doesn't, the work has to be done under a company that does: that decides who contracts with the family and whose certificate is in the handover pack | Certification body | **Settings → Company** → F-gas certificate; checklist item "Company F-gas certificate" (statutory: it holds install day 1, not the booking) |
| 17 | **F-gas Category I** for every engineer who handles refrigerant | Legal requirement | Each engineer's certification body | **Staff** → qualification, evidence, ticked as checked. Refrigerant steps and commissioning stay locked without it |
| 18 | **Part P**: the electrician is registered with a competent person scheme (for example NICEIC or NAPIT) | A new circuit in a home is notifiable building work. Without a scheme member, a building notice must go to the council before work starts | The electrician's scheme | **Staff** → "Part P competent person scheme". No booking can be confirmed without it |
| 18a | **Supply capacity and the network operator (DNO)** for each job: cut-out rating, looped service, earthing, existing large loads and maximum demand; then connect-and-notify within 28 days, or apply first | BS 7671 132.16 (the existing equipment, the distributor's included, must be adequate); the ENA process for heat pumps. Whether UK Power Networks treats an air-to-air unit as a heat pump wasn't confirmed: ask them once | The electrician (UK Power Networks for the route) | Checklist: **Supply capacity and the network operator (DNO)**: done before booking, and it holds install day 1 until the electrician writes the route (notified with the reference, applied with the reference and the answer, or not needed and why). The connect-and-notify criteria in its guidance (a maximum demand limit, the ENA's model list) are from search extracts of the ENA forms: verify against the current one |
| 18b | **The circuit's design**: RCD type for the outdoor unit (Type A, F or B, from its manual), cable size for the route, armoured cable outside, the interconnect cable | BS 7671 (531.3.3 for the RCD type, Appendix 4 for cable ratings); an inverter can produce residual currents (high-frequency, or smooth DC) a Type A device isn't designed for; the Heat Pump Association's 2025 guidance, read through search extracts, says Type F or B | The electrician | The circuit step on day 2; the design's figures are only a starting point |
| 19 | **Building Regulations for the heat pump itself**: which competent person scheme covers the installation, or the council's approval before work starts (a building notice for a house; full plans in most blocks of flats; the Building Safety Regulator for a higher-risk building outside a scheme) | The installation is likely notifiable in its own right; the compliance certificate must reach the family within 30 days, operating information within 5 days, and building control must be notified within 30 days | Building control or your scheme | Checklist: the route before day 1; **Building Regulations certificate for the heat pump, to the customer** opens only once the install is complete and approves only with the certificate attached and its number (the family's page then says "Sent to you"); the handover pack and the family's page list it separately from the circuit's |
| 20 | **Manufacturer accreditation and warranty terms in writing** for each brand you fit (Panasonic PRO Club, Daikin Certified Installer and Stand By Me, Mitsubishi Accredited Installer) | Extended warranties usually need an accredited installer and registration within a deadline (Daikin: 90 days). No warranty years are shown anywhere until you set them | Manufacturer or distributor | **Settings → Warranty**; each item's warranty in the **Catalogue** |
| 21 | **Commissioning targets** from each model's installation manual: nitrogen test pressure (the manual's, never above the nameplate PS), the pressure hold time and any allowed fall, vacuum target (if stricter than 500 micron), the decay hold time and rise limit, flare torque per pipe size (and for an indoor unit with no pipe sizes on record), any multi-split reducers and their torque (and each multi-split's port sizes in the catalogue), condensate fall; the leak check method (an electronic detector, 5 g a year, at a quarter of PS or more) and the brazing procedure (nitrogen flowing) | None was verified in the research. The pressure and vacuum tests are judged from their readings meanwhile against the company's defaults (no fall beyond the gauge's resolution after temperature correction; 500 micron), but the hold times and the rise limit have no default, and commissioning can't be signed until every target is set or confirmed | Technical lead | Field app → **Commission** tab → **Set target** |
| 22 | **RAMS, the construction phase plan and working at height** for each job (towers, ladders; PASMA or IPAF where needed; hot work for a brazed joint) | Health and safety duties; CDM 2015 passes a domestic client's duties to the contractor (a construction phase plan, induction, welfare; with a subcontractor, principal contractor and designer and a health and safety file) | Technical lead / you | Checklist items "Risk assessment and method statement" (its approval names the principal contractor and designer) and "Working at height plan"; crew acknowledge RAMS in the job pack; with a subcontractor, the handover pack's health and safety file and the technical lead's line at commissioning |
| 23 | **Asbestos survey** where it's suspected | Never drill suspected asbestos | Licensed asbestos surveyor | Raised as a safety issue by itself at submit; the checklist item "Asbestos check before drilling" approves only with the result (or the avoiding route) attached, and can be marked not applicable only for a home the survey dates 2000 or later with nothing suspected |
| 24 | **Authority to contract**: the person signing owns the home, or has the owner's permission in writing (the landlord's for a tenant, the freeholder's or managing agent's for a leaseholder) | A tenant can't agree to holes in someone else's wall; most leases need the freeholder's consent for the outside wall | You, at the survey | Survey: tenure and "Owner, or authority to go ahead, confirmed"; for a leaseholder or tenant, the checklist item needs the written consent attached and holds day 1 |
| 24a | **Equipment delivered to the family's home before day 1**: who carries the risk while it waits there (two weeks or more, so the cancellation period can run from delivery), and whether our insurance covers our kit at a customer's home | CRA 2015 s29 (goods stay at the trader's risk until the consumer has physical possession; read through a legislation.gov.uk search extract: verify) and s31 (a term can't shift it further). The kit emails now ask the family to be in for the delivery and keep the boxes unopened and dry, but say nothing about risk | Solicitor and the insurance broker | **Kit** → **Deliver to** (the family's home by default, or our storage: then the period runs from day 1 and an early start needs their request); the family's prep list has "Take in the equipment delivery" |
| 25 | **Early-start request in writing** for any install inside the cancellation period | Consumer Contracts Regulations reg 36 | The family (their reply email) | Booking page: early-start evidence; dates inside the period are refused without it |
